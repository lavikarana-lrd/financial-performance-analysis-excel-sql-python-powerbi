📊 Financial & Retail Analytics Dashboard
Driving Strategic Fiscal Decisions Through Interactive Data
📝 Short Description
This project is an end-to-end data analytics solution that transforms raw corporate data into a dynamic Power BI dashboard. It provides executive leadership with a consolidated, interactive view of the company's financial health, tracking everything from revenue variances and operational expenses to vendor spend and customer demographics.

🎯 Purpose of Analysis
The primary purpose of this analysis is to:

Compare actual financial performance against baseline budgets to uncover variances.

Identify the primary drivers of corporate expenses and operational costs.

Evaluate the performance of different business segments (Online, Retail, Enterprise).

Provide a "single source of truth" for leadership to make data-driven strategic decisions.

🛠️ Tools Used
Power BI: Used for data modeling, DAX measure creation, and building the interactive visual dashboard.

SQL / Python: Used for initial data extraction, cleaning, and exploratory data analysis (EDA).

GitHub: Used for version control, portfolio hosting, and project documentation.

📂 Data Source
The analysis is built on internal corporate datasets consisting of 5 core relational tables:

Financial_Transactions (Revenue & Expenses)

Budget (Forecasted targets)

Customers (Client segments and regions)

Headcount (Employee payroll and status)

Vendors (Supply chain and partnership spend)

✨ Features & Highlights
Dynamic Filtering: Users can slice data globally by Year, Month, and Business Unit using the interactive left-hand panel.

Variance Tracking: Real-time calculation of how actual numbers perform against forecasted budgets.

360-Degree View: Combines HR data (Headcount), Supply Chain (Vendors), and Sales (Customers) into one financial view.

🚨 The Business Problem
Prior to this dashboard, the company lacked a centralized system to track financial performance in real-time. Leadership was struggling to answer critical questions: Are we hitting our budget? Where are our highest operational costs coming from? Which business unit is actually driving the most revenue? This lack of visibility made it difficult to manage risks and allocate resources effectively.

🥅 Goal of the Dashboard
To turn thousands of complex financial transaction logs into a "decision-ready" visual tool that allows executives to spot trends, detect anomalies, and optimize budgets in seconds, without needing technical data skills.

📈 Key Visuals & Their Purpose
Based on the dashboard layout, here is how the visuals answer critical business questions:

KPI Scorecards (Top Row): * What it shows: Total Budgeted Revenue, Revenue Variance, Budgeted Expense, and Expense Variance.

Why: Gives executives an immediate, 5-second pulse check on the overall financial health and budget alignment.

Line Chart - Revenue Trend Over Time (Top Left):

What it shows: Chronological spikes and dips in revenue.

Why: Helps identify seasonal sales patterns and long-term growth trajectories.

Donut Chart - Expense Breakdown (Top Middle):

What it shows: Proportion of spending across categories (Rent, Marketing, Utilities, Payroll, etc.).

Why: Pinpoints exactly where the company's money is going, highlighting the biggest targets for cost-cutting.

Matrix Table - Employee Headcount & Cost (Top Right):

What it shows: Individual and departmental payroll costs.

Why: Allows HR and Finance to monitor labor costs and ensure workforce spending is efficient.

Bar Chart - Revenue by Segment (Bottom Left):

What it shows: Compares revenue generated by Online, Retail, and Enterprise units.

Why: Identifies the most profitable business units to guide future investment and marketing focus.

Bar Chart - Vendor Spend Analysis (Bottom Middle):

What it shows: Total capital spent per individual vendor.

Why: Highlights which third-party partners cost the most, aiding procurement in contract renegotiations or vendor consolidation.

Column Chart - Customer Demographics (Bottom Right):

What it shows: Distribution of customers across geographical regions (East, West, North, South).

Why: Informs regional sales teams and marketing departments where their strongest (and weakest) markets are located.

💼 Business Impact & Derivatives
Budget Recalibration: By exposing massive positive revenue variances, the company can confidently set more aggressive growth targets for the next fiscal year.

Cost Optimization: Granular visibility into Vendor Spend and Expense Breakdowns empowers the procurement team to consolidate vendors and negotiate better rates.

Resource Allocation: Proving that the "Online" segment is a major revenue driver justifies shifting marketing budgets to support digital growth.
